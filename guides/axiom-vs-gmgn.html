Axiom vs GMGN: compare the costs and the workflow

Sources checked 16 October 2026 · By FuturesEdge

Choose the workflow you need, then compare the actual cost of your order. A single headline fee does not capture transaction costs, execution or later rewards.

Question Axiom GMGN
Documented fee structure Tier-based net fees and cashback; published net rates range from 0.95% to 0.75%. The copy-trading guide describes a 1% handling fee plus gas priority costs.
Useful workflow to investigate Its market-order guide explains buying, slippage, priority fees and MEV protection. Its copy-trading guide explains wallet tasks, follow-selling and take-profit/stop-loss rules.
What else affects cost? Priority fees, MEV tips, venue costs and execution. Priority costs, repeated copied orders and execution differences.
Before you compare Separate later cashback from the immediate charge. Use your own copied position size and selected sell mode.

Sources: Axiom fees, Axiom market orders and GMGN copy trading. The documented workflows above are examples, not a complete feature inventory or a claim of exclusivity.

Compare like with like

Axiom's net rate after cashback and GMGN's stated handling fee are different measures. Compare the same order size, chain, venue and timing. Check the immediate charge first and account for any reward separately after confirming eligibility and receipt.

Run your own cost scenario

Use your quoted fee rates, not a preset ranking. The calculator treats fixed costs as one total across the round trip, and slippage as an estimated execution loss rather than the tolerance setting.

Which should you investigate first?

If you want to place your own orders, review Axiom's order-entry workflow. If following wallets is central to your process, review GMGN's copy-task controls. Test the specific functions you need; neither a feature list nor a lower quoted fee establishes future trading performance.
