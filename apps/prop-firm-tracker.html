Why a fuel gauge beats a spreadsheet

Most challenge failures aren't a bad week of trading — they're a rule the trader lost track of mid-session: a drawdown floor that trails on some firms and locks on others, a daily loss limit that's soft on one plan and hard on the next, a consistency rule that caps how much of your profit can come from a single best day. Every prop firm encodes these differently, and the differences are exactly where challenges get breached. Prop Firm Challenge Tracker builds each firm's actual rule mechanics in — static, end-of-day trailing, intraday trailing, and locking drawdowns are all computed correctly, not approximated.

An honest limitation

Buffers are computed from your logged end-of-day figures — this app can't see your broker or prop firm account directly. It's a discipline tool that turns each firm's published rules into a live buffer, not a broker connection or an automated feed. You log the number; it does the rule math.
