Why keep a trading journal at all

Most traders can tell you their last few trades from memory. Almost none can tell you their actual win rate, their average R-multiple, or whether their Tuesday trades are quietly losing money while their Friday trades carry the account. A spreadsheet can get you there, eventually, if you keep it up — but most people don't. FuturesEdge Crypto Journal exists to make that record-keeping fast enough that you actually do it, and detailed enough that it tells you something a memory can't: the patterns hiding in your own trading history.

It's built for crypto, futures and forex traders specifically — the entry, exit, size, leverage and R-multiple fields all map to how leveraged and spot trading actually works, rather than being a generic notes app with a P&L column bolted on.

What's inside

  • Fast trade logging Entry, exit, size, P&L, R-multiple, setup and emotion tags, notes and screenshots
  • Performance dashboard Net P&L, win rate, average R, profit factor, equity curve and monthly breakdowns
  • Insight tools Day-of-week heatmap, R-multiple distribution, and win/loss patterns
  • Built-in position size calculator Risk %, R:R, leverage cap and target levels, without leaving the journal
  • Import from 14 exchanges Binance, Bybit, OKX, Bitget, MEXC, Kraken, Coinbase, KuCoin, Gate.io and more, via CSV/XLSX
  • Export your data anytime CSV and PDF, plus full backup and restore
  • Monthly goal tracking Set a target and watch progress alongside your real stats

Private by design

Your trades never leave your device. There's no account to create, no login screen, and no cloud sync — all data is stored locally, and uninstalling the app removes everything with it. For a niche where the app is asking you to record real money numbers, that's a genuine feature rather than a limitation: nothing you log here can be breached, sold, or scraped, because it was never sent anywhere in the first place.

Who it's for

Crypto and futures traders who want a simple, honest record of their trading — to spot their best setups, cut their worst habits, and build consistency over time. If you've outgrown "I think I'm profitable" and want the dashboard that actually proves or disproves it, this is built for exactly that moment.

Frequently asked questions

Does my trade data ever leave my device?

No. FuturesEdge Crypto Journal is private by design — there's no account, no login and no cloud sync. Everything you log is stored locally on your device, and uninstalling the app removes everything.

Which exchanges can I import trade history from?

14 exchanges via CSV or XLSX import, including Binance, Bybit, OKX, Bitget, MEXC, Kraken, Coinbase, KuCoin and Gate.io.

Is there a free trial for FuturesEdge Pro?

Yes — Pro unlocks unlimited trade logging, full performance analytics, pattern insights and data export, with a free trial before the subscription (billed monthly or yearly) begins.

Can I get my data out of the app?

Yes — export anytime as CSV or PDF, and use backup/restore to move your journal between devices.
