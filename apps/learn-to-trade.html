Before you start

What can I try for free?

The first three lessons in each learning path are free: six introductory lessons in total. An optional one-time purchase unlocks the remaining lessons in both courses. See Google Play and the app for your local price.

Is this a trading account or broker?

No. This is an educational app. It does not execute trades or hold funds. Exercises use fictional scenarios and stated assumptions.

Does my progress sync between devices?

No. Learning progress is saved locally on your device. Google Play and RevenueCat process purchases to manage paid course access.
