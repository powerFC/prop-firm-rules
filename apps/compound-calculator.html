Why growth, risk, EV and survival belong on the same screen

Compound growth calculators are everywhere, and they're seductive for a reason — a modest per-period gain, compounded for a year, produces a chart that climbs into numbers that feel inevitable. What most of those calculators leave out is the other half of the same math: the same aggressive risk-per-trade that makes the growth curve steep is very often the exact setting that makes ruin likely, not unlikely. Prop Firm Calculator puts growth and risk in front of you at once, then goes further — before you pay for an evaluation, the EV Calculator tells you whether the pass rate and payout on offer are actually worth the entry fee, and the Survival Simulator runs your specific numbers against a specific firm's drawdown rules thousands of times to show you the real odds, not a marketing pass rate.

How the Risk of Ruin card differs from the Survival Simulator

Risk of Ruin isn't a trade-by-trade simulation — it's the classical closed-form gambler's-ruin result, applied by approximating fixed-fractional risk (a percentage of a balance that itself grows and shrinks trade to trade) as a fixed number of equal capital units. It's a well-established approximation, not a guess, and it comes with an honest caveat: real risk of ruin, once you account for position sizing that compounds and a sensible stopping rule instead of trading forever, is generally lower than what this card shows. The Survival Simulator is the more precise tool for a specific prop firm challenge — it runs a full Monte Carlo simulation against that firm's actual drawdown mechanics rather than a general approximation.

Frequently asked questions

Is the risk of ruin number an exact simulation?

The Risk of Ruin card uses the classical closed-form gambler's-ruin approximation, treating fixed-fractional risk as a fixed number of equal capital units, rather than a full Monte Carlo simulation. The Survival Simulator, by contrast, runs a full Monte Carlo engine against a specific firm's drawdown rules.

What does the Survival Simulator actually simulate?

It runs thousands of simulated challenge attempts using your win rate and risk per trade against a specific prop firm's drawdown rules, then reports your pass rate, breach rate, and where in the attempt failures typically happen.

What does the EV Calculator tell you that a pass rate doesn't?

Given an evaluation's cost, pass rate, payout chance and payout size, it works out your average cost to get funded, the expected value per evaluation, your return multiple, and how many accounts you'd need to hit a target monthly payout.

Does the app store my numbers anywhere?

No. All calculations stay on your device — no account, no cloud, no data collected.
