Simulated scenarios, rather than live-market paper trading

The charts are fictional training scenarios, not historical token replays or live prices. The app does not connect to a wallet or place real trades. Its scenario mix is not a measure of real-market probabilities.

Practice execution is simplified. It cannot reproduce live liquidity, network conditions or every trading cost. Simulated performance does not establish a trading edge or predict live results.

Questions before you start

Is it free?

The app is free to download, and the included practice round is replayable. Additional random practice rounds require a one-time purchase. Check Google Play and the app for the price in your currency.

Does this use real token prices?

No. Practice uses fictional scenarios with simulated price paths. You do not need a wallet or real funds.

Can I practise on iPhone or in a browser?

The practice app is available on Android. The separate browser break-even calculator works without installing the app; it is a calculation tool, not the practice simulator.
