Why meme coin math needs its own calculator

A generic position calculator assumes numbers like "market cap" and "target price" behave the way they do on a normal chart. Meme coin positions don't work that way — market caps are quoted in shorthand, a target multiple means something different depending on the market cap you actually bought at, and the fees and slippage on small, illiquid positions eat a share of the trade that a spreadsheet built for stocks or futures never accounts for. This app does that specific math, and only that math — no wallet, no live chart, no addresses, nothing that touches an actual token.

What's inside

  • Market cap multiple Enter the market cap you bought at, your position size in USD, and a target market cap (type it or drag the slider) — get position value at target, profit or loss in dollars, the multiple, and percentage change, all updating live. Shorthand like 40k, 40K, 40000 and 1.2m all parse correctly
  • Break-even after fees Buy fee, sell fee and slippage as percentages, returning your total round-trip cost and the market cap your position needs to reach just to break even
  • Hit rate and expectancy Enter your hit rate and average winner multiple to get your expectancy per trade and the break-even multiple your winners need to clear
  • Rug tells A plain-English reference of mechanics worth checking before buying — written as patterns to recognise, not predictions about any specific token
  • Trade log A local record of trades you've taken, including the ones that went to zero, so your hit rate comes from your actual history instead of memory
  • Private by design Everything is calculated and stored on your device — no wallet, no addresses, no live token data, no account

What this app doesn't do

It doesn't connect to a wallet, doesn't read or generate addresses, doesn't pull live token data, and doesn't place, route or recommend trades. Every figure it produces comes directly from the numbers you type in, and none of it leaves your device. It's a calculator and a private log — not a terminal, not a tracker, and not a signal.

Frequently asked questions

What does the market cap multiple calculator actually compute?

You enter the market cap you bought at, your position size in USD, and a target market cap. It returns your position's value at that target, your profit or loss in dollars, the multiple, and the percentage change — updating live as you move the target slider.

Does the app connect to a wallet or track live prices?

No. There's no wallet connection, no addresses, and no live token data of any kind — every number comes from what you type in, and every calculation happens on your device.

What counts as "break-even" in the fees calculator?

You enter your buy fee, sell fee and expected slippage as percentages. The calculator totals the real cost of the round trip and translates it into the market cap your position actually needs to reach before you're even — not the market cap you bought at.

Can I log a trade that went to zero?

Yes — the trade log is built to capture losers, including full washouts, alongside winners. Hit rate and expectancy mean something when they come from your logged history rather than the trades you remember.
