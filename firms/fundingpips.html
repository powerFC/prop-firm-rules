How FundingPips' drawdown works

Every FundingPips plan is modeled as a static drawdown — a fixed percentage of your starting balance, set once and never adjusted regardless of how your balance moves. The percentage itself varies a lot by plan, from a tight 5% on Zero up to a relatively loose 12% on 2 Step Flex — the plan you pick changes your risk budget significantly even though the mechanic is the same across all five.

Frequently asked questions

Which FundingPips plan has no minimum trading days?

2 Step Flex — it's the only plan among FundingPips' five with 0 minimum trading days. Every other plan requires at least 1 (Pro) or 3 (1 Step, 2 Step Standard) trading days.

Does FundingPips Zero have a consistency rule?

Yes — the only FundingPips plan that does. Your best single day can't exceed 15% of your total profit, and you need at least 7 profitable days before you're eligible for a payout.

Is FundingPips' drawdown static or trailing?

All five plans are modeled as static in our data, but this isn't officially confirmed on FundingPips' own site — treat it as our best assumption pending direct confirmation at fundingpips.com.
