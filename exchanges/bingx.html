Leverage

BingX's own position-and-leverage tier table lists a maximum of 150× on the BTC-USDT perpetual contract, available on position notional value up to 300,000 USDT, stepping down through 13 tiers to 1× as position size grows into the billions. The cap on smaller, less liquid pairs is lower than BTC-USDT's.

Markets

BingX's product range spans spot, USDⓢ-M, Coin-M and USDC-M perpetual futures, Standard Futures, TradFi products (stocks, indices, metals and forex via CFD — BingX states up to 1000× leverage on these specifically, separate from its crypto futures caps above), a trading bot suite (grid, Martingale, spot infinity grid), and both spot and futures copy trading with a public leaderboard and an elite-trader recruitment program.

Who BingX suits

Traders who want to follow or copy other traders' strategies as a core part of how they trade — BingX's leaderboard and elite-trader program are a bigger part of its product than on the other two exchanges compared here. Its published base futures taker fee is also the lowest of the three, which suits active futures traders sensitive to per-trade cost.
