The figures above are Bitget's base rate — the tier every new account starts on before any volume-based VIP discount or BGB token fee discount is applied. Bitget also runs USDC-M and Coin-M futures at the same base maker/taker rate as USDT-M. Fee tiers scale down substantially at higher 30-day trading volumes; check Bitget's own VIP fee table for your actual rate once you're trading.

Leverage

Bitget's contract details page lists a maximum leverage of 150× on the BTC/USDT perpetual contract. Maximum leverage is tiered by position size — the full 150× is only available on smaller position notional values, stepping down as your position grows, and the cap varies by trading pair (smaller, less liquid pairs carry a lower maximum than BTC/USDT).

Markets

Bitget's product range spans spot, USDT-M / USDC-M / Coin-M futures, margin trading, CFDs on stocks, gold and other commodities (its "TradFi" / Universal Exchange offering), on-chain trading, plus spot and futures copy trading and a trading bot suite. Bitget states 790+ futures trading pairs and over 800 tradeable assets across the platform.

Who Bitget suits

Traders who want crypto, stocks and commodity CFDs available from a single account, without switching platforms to trade a gold or an index position alongside a crypto one. The combination of a competitive base futures fee, a high published leverage cap, and copy trading / bot tooling also suits traders who want automation options built in rather than bolted on through a third party.
