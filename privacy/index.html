Privacy Policy

This policy covers both the FuturesEdge website (futuresedgetrade.app) and the FuturesEdge mobile apps — Trading Journal, Prop Firm Challenge Tracker, Position Size Calculator, Prop Firm Calculator and Meme Coin Trenches — on both iOS and Android.

The FuturesEdge apps

The apps do not collect your personal data. Every FuturesEdge app is an on-device tool. There is no account, no sign-up and no login, and we have no server that stores your information.

  • Everything you enter stays on your device. Trades, challenges, positions, calculations and settings are saved in the app's own local storage. None of it is transmitted to us or to anyone else, and we cannot see it.
  • No analytics or tracking. The apps contain no analytics SDK, no advertising SDK and no crash-reporting service that profiles you.
  • Backups stay yours. Where an app offers backup and restore, it writes a file you choose the destination for. We never receive a copy.
  • Deleting the app deletes the data. Because nothing is stored off-device, uninstalling removes it. We hold no copy to delete on request.

Purchases

The iOS versions contain no in-app purchases and make no network requests of any kind — no data leaves your device.

Some Android versions offer a paid unlock. Those purchases are processed by Google Play, and entitlement is checked through RevenueCat, which receives an anonymous purchase identifier so the app knows what you've bought. Neither receives your name, email, or anything you've entered into the app, and we never see your payment details — Google Play handles payment entirely.

Links out of the apps

The apps link to prop firms, exchanges and trading terminals, and some of those are affiliate links — where they appear, the app says so on screen. Tapping one opens your device's browser. Once you leave the app, that company's privacy policy applies, not this one.

What this site collects by default

Nothing. Every page on futuresedgetrade.app other than the free guide page is static content — there's no analytics, no tracking script, and no cookies set by anything we've built.

The free guide page

If you enter your email on the Prop Firm Challenge Guide page to receive the free guide, we collect:

  • Your email address — nothing else is asked for.

We use it to send you the guide, and occasionally to let you know when FuturesEdge ships something new. That's it — we don't sell or share your email with anyone outside the email provider below.

That form is processed by Kit (formerly ConvertKit), our email service provider — your email address is stored and the emails are sent through their platform, so Kit's own privacy policy also applies to how they handle it. Kit's embedded signup widget may set its own cookies to operate the form; those aren't controlled by us.

Unsubscribing: every email we send includes an unsubscribe link. Click it any time and you'll be removed immediately — no need to ask us first.

Affiliate and outbound links

Some links on this site (to exchanges and prop trading firms) are affiliate links — see the disclosure on the page you found them on. Once you leave futuresedgetrade.app for one of those sites, or for Google Play, their own privacy policy applies, not this one.

Contact

Questions about this policy, or want your email address removed sooner than an unsubscribe link allows? Email futuresedgetrade@gmail.com.
