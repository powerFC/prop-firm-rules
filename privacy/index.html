Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 October 2026

This policy covers both the FuturesEdge website (futuresedgetrade.app) and the FuturesEdge mobile apps — Trading Journal, Prop Firm Challenge Tracker, Position Size Calculator, Prop Firm Calculator and Meme Coin Trading Practice (previously Meme Coin Profit & Break-Even, also known as Meme Coin Trenches) — on both iOS and Android.

The FuturesEdge apps

The apps use local storage for your trading tools. No FuturesEdge app account or login is required. Purchase-related information is processed by Google Play and RevenueCat as described below; your calculator entries, trade logs and practice results stay local.

  • Your tool data stays on your device. Trades, challenges, positions, calculations, practice results and settings are saved in the app's local storage. These entries are not sent to FuturesEdge or RevenueCat.
  • No advertising tracking. The apps do not use an advertising tracking SDK or collect advertising identifiers. Purchase analytics are described below.
  • Backups stay yours. Where an app offers backup and restore, it writes a file you choose the destination for. We never receive a copy.
  • Deleting the app removes its local data. Device backups may retain a copy under your device provider's settings. Uninstalling does not delete app-store or RevenueCat purchase records.

Purchases

Purchase availability varies by app and platform. Where a paid Android feature is offered, payment is processed by Google Play and access is validated through RevenueCat.

RevenueCat processes an anonymous customer identifier and purchase records, including product identifiers, purchase tokens or receipts and entitlement status. These records provide and restore paid access and support purchase analytics, such as purchases and refunds. Data is encrypted in transit. RevenueCat acts as our purchase-processing service provider. We do not sell this data or use purchase records for targeted advertising.

We do not send RevenueCat your name, email, phone number, calculator entries or trade logs. FuturesEdge does not receive your payment-card or bank details. Google Play handles payment information under Google's privacy policy.

Purchase records are retained while needed to validate and restore paid access, subject to deletion requests and applicable legal retention requirements. Uninstalling the app does not cancel a purchase or delete these records.

Purchase-data deletion and support

To request deletion of purchase-related customer records held through RevenueCat, email futuresedgetrade@gmail.com and identify the app and relevant Google Play order. Do not send payment-card details, passwords, seed phrases or private keys. We may need enough information to locate and verify the record. Deleting entitlement records may affect automatic restoration of the paid feature. Google Play retains its own payment records under its policies; contact Google for requests concerning those records.

Meme Coin Trading Practice

Meme Coin Trading Practice is developed by FuturesEdge. The Android app offers an optional one-time purchase for unlimited generated practice rounds, processed by Google Play and RevenueCat as described above. Calculations, settings, trade logs and practice results remain on your device. There is no app account or login.

The app does not connect a wallet or request a wallet address, seed phrase, private key or exchange API key. Practice charts are generated simulations, not live token feeds. The app does not execute real-money trades.

Android may check whether specific FuturesEdge apps are installed, solely to show or hide their links. It does not scan your full installed-app list. These checks are used locally and are not transmitted to us.

The app links to trading platforms, our website and other apps. Following a link opens an external service governed by its own privacy policy. Some links are affiliate links, and FuturesEdge may earn a commission. Local practice and calculator records are not attached to these links. This app is not directed at anyone under 18.

We may update this policy; the updated policy and date will appear on this page.

Links out of the apps

The apps link to prop firms, exchanges and trading terminals, and some of those are affiliate links — where they appear, the app says so on screen. Tapping one opens your device's browser. Once you leave the app, that company's privacy policy applies, not this one.

What this site collects by default

Nothing. Every page on futuresedgetrade.app other than the free guide page is static content — there's no analytics, no tracking script, and no cookies set by anything we've built.

The free guide page

If you enter your email on the Prop Firm Challenge Guide page to receive the free guide, we collect:

  • Your email address — nothing else is asked for.

We use it to send you the guide, and occasionally to let you know when FuturesEdge ships something new. That's it — we don't sell or share your email with anyone outside the email provider below.

That form is processed by Kit (formerly ConvertKit), our email service provider — your email address is stored and the emails are sent through their platform, so Kit's own privacy policy also applies to how they handle it. Kit's embedded signup widget may set its own cookies to operate the form; those aren't controlled by us.

Unsubscribing: every email we send includes an unsubscribe link. Click it any time and you'll be removed immediately — no need to ask us first.

Affiliate and outbound links

Some links on this site (to exchanges and prop trading firms) are affiliate links — see the disclosure on the page you found them on. Once you leave futuresedgetrade.app for one of those sites, or for Google Play, their own privacy policy applies, not this one.

Contact

Questions about this policy, or want your email address removed sooner than an unsubscribe link allows? Email futuresedgetrade@gmail.com.
