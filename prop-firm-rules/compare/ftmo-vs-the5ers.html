FTMO vs The5%ers (2026)

✓ Checked against official rules 20 November 2026

FTMO 1-Step and The5%ers Summer 1-Step both have a 10% evaluation target. Compare how each sets the daily loss and maximum-loss floors, and check which consistency calculation applies.

How the loss limits work

On a $100,000 simulated account, FTMO's maximum-loss floor starts at $90,000. If its balance reaches $104,000 at midnight CE(S)T, the floor rises to $94,000 and stays there if a later reset balance is lower. The5%ers Summer 1-Step starts with a $94,000 maximum-loss floor, fixed from the initial balance. See FTMO Trading Objectives and the The5%ers Summer Plan rules.

FTMO subtracts a fixed daily loss amount from the balance at the daily reset. The5%ers' Summer Plan page says its daily loss amount uses the higher of end-of-day equity or balance. Its separate 1-Step specification refers to the previous day's closing balance. Check the terms for your account if those values differ.

How consistency is calculated

FTMO current rule. For accounts initiated from orders created on or after 12 October 2026, or merged on or after that date, the best day's closed profit can be at most 50% of total closed profit. Losing days reduce that total.

FTMO legacy rule. Accounts directly initiated from earlier orders use the legacy Best Day Rule, subject to the merger rule above. The calculation uses only the net profit of profitable days. For example, daily results of +$6,000, −$2,000 and +$6,000 put the best day at 60% under the current rule and 50% under the legacy rule.

The5%ers Summer 1-Step. The plan confirms a 50% best-day cap. Its formula FAQ refers to profits from profitable days. A separate consistency FAQ says it adds winning trades only when calculating daily consistency P&L. Check the calculation for your plan before estimating how a day with both winning and losing trades will count.

Frequently asked questions

How do the two maximum-loss floors work?

On a $100,000 simulated account, the FTMO 1-Step floor starts at $90,000 and can rise with the highest midnight balance. The5%ers Summer 1-Step floor stays at $94,000 because its 6% maximum loss is measured from the initial balance.

How do FTMO and The5%ers' consistency rules differ?

FTMO's current 1-Step rule uses total closed profit, including losing days. Its legacy Best Day Rule uses profit from profitable days only. The5%ers confirms a 50% cap for Summer 1-Step. Its general FAQs refer to profits from profitable days and say losing trades are excluded from daily consistency P&L.
