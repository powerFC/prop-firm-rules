How FTMO's drawdown works

2-Step: a static floor. The maximum loss limit is 10% of initial capital and does not trail profits. This applies to both evaluation phases and the subsequent 2-Step FTMO Account.

1-Step: an end-of-day trailing floor. At 00:00 CE(S)T, FTMO subtracts 10% of initial capital from the highest preceding midnight balance or initial capital, whichever is higher. On a $100,000 account the floor starts at $90,000; a $105,000 midnight balance raises it to $95,000. It never decreases during that account. FTMO's Trading Objectives and comparison table confirm the distinction, checked 8 October 2026.

Frequently asked questions

Is FTMO's max drawdown static or trailing?

The 2-Step uses a static maximum loss limit of 10% of initial capital in both evaluation phases and on the subsequent FTMO Account. On a $100,000 account that floor stays at $90,000. The 1-Step uses an end-of-day trailing limit: the highest preceding midnight balance, or initial capital if higher, minus 10% of initial capital. A $105,000 midnight balance moves the 1-Step floor to $95,000 permanently.

Does FTMO have a consistency rule?

Not on the 2-Step Evaluation. The 1-Step Evaluation has a Best Day Rule: your single best day of profit can't exceed 50% of the total profit from your positive days.

Is the FTMO evaluation fee refundable?

On the 2-Step Evaluation, yes — it's refunded once you pass. On the 1-Step Evaluation, the fee is non-refundable, per our verified notes.
