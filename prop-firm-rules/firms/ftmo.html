How FTMO's drawdown works

Both plans use an end-of-day trailing floor. FTMO recalculates your maximum loss limit at 00:00 CE(S)T each day, measured from the highest balance recorded at 00:00 on any preceding trading day — or your initial capital, whichever is higher. In FTMO's own words the limit "can only increase, but never decrease". On a $100,000 account the floor starts at $90,000. Close one day at $105,000 and the floor moves permanently to $95,000, even if you give the profit back. This is the mechanic most often assumed to work the other way, and it applies to both phases of the 2-Step as well as the funded FTMO Account.

Frequently asked questions

Is FTMO's max drawdown static or trailing?

Trailing, on both plans. FTMO recalculates the maximum loss limit daily from the highest account balance at 00:00 on any preceding trading day, and in FTMO's words the limit "can only increase, but never decrease". On a $100,000 account your floor starts at $90,000, but if you close a day at $105,000 the floor moves permanently to $95,000. This applies to both phases of the 2-Step Evaluation and to the 1-Step.

Does FTMO have a consistency rule?

Not on the 2-Step Evaluation. The 1-Step Evaluation has a Best Day Rule: your single best day of profit can't exceed 50% of the total profit from your positive days.

Is the FTMO evaluation fee refundable?

On the 2-Step Evaluation, yes — it's refunded once you pass. On the 1-Step Evaluation, the fee is non-refundable, per our verified notes.
