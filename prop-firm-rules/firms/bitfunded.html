How Bitfunded's drawdown works

Every Bitfunded plan is modeled as a static drawdown in our data — a fixed percentage of your starting balance, set once and never adjusted. Bitfunded's own plan tables don't explicitly confirm this (they simply don't state whether the floor trails), so treat it as our best current assumption rather than an official guarantee.

Notes & unconfirmed details

  • Max drawdown type (static vs. trailing) is not stated on Bitfunded's plan tables — encoded here as static pending confirmation.
  • The 2-Step's funded (Trader Stage) limits mirror Phase 2's exactly; the 1-Step's funded limits mirror its evaluation exactly — neither is modeled as a separate phase above beyond what's shown.

Frequently asked questions

Is Bitfunded's max drawdown static or trailing?

Our data encodes it as static across all three plans, but Bitfunded's own plan tables don't explicitly state whether it trails — treat this as our best current assumption pending confirmation at bitfunded.com.

What happens to Bitfunded's rules once I'm funded?

They carry over almost unchanged. On the 2-Step, the funded Trader Stage's daily loss and max drawdown exactly match Phase 2's limits (5% / 8%). On the 1-Step, the funded stage exactly matches the evaluation's limits (4% / 6%).

Is there a time limit on a Bitfunded evaluation?

No — none of the three plans (2-Step, 1-Step, Instant) carry a time limit, and none require a minimum number of trading days.
