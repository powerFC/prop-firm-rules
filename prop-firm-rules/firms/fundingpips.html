How FundingPips' drawdown works

The evaluation models use static maximum-loss floors. Zero is different: its floor trails the highest recorded equity with a buffer of 5% of initial account size. Once equity reaches 5% above the start, the floor locks at starting balance and never moves down.

Frequently asked questions

Which FundingPips plan has no minimum trading days?

Current 1 Step Flex and new or reset 2 Step Pro accounts have no minimum trading days. The 3% daily-loss add-on also removes the minimum on 2 Step Standard. Current 2 Step Flex requires one trading day on the 80% split or three profitable days on the 95% split. Older accounts may retain different conditions.

Does FundingPips Zero have a consistency rule?

Yes. Your best single day cannot exceed 15% of total cumulative profit. Reward eligibility also requires seven days with at least 0.25% profit each within the rolling 30-day period; other reward conditions apply.

Is FundingPips' drawdown static or trailing?

The evaluation models use static drawdown. Zero trails the highest recorded equity with a buffer of 5% of initial account size and locks permanently at starting balance once equity is 5% above the start.
