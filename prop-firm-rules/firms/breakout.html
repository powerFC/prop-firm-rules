Breakout Rules Explained (2026)

✓ Rules verified 2026-07-15

Breakout — the Kraken-owned crypto prop firm — runs three single-step plans (Classic, Pro, Turbo) plus a 2-Step, all with no minimum trading days, no consistency rule and no time limit. The 2-Step's trailing floor locking at your starting balance is the one mechanic worth understanding closely.

No minimum trading days, no consistency rule, and no time limit on any Breakout plan. Account sizes: $5,000–$100,000 on Classic and 2-Step; $5,000–$200,000 on Pro and Turbo. Leverage is 5:1 on BTC/ETH and 2:1 on altcoins; only one evaluation can be active at a time. Weekend holding and news-event trading are both allowed. Fees are 0.035% per side plus a 0.05%/day swap charge.

How Breakout's drawdown works

Classic, Pro and Turbo all use a static drawdown — a fixed percentage of your starting balance that never moves. Their daily loss limit is different from the drawdown, though: it's calculated as a percentage of your balance at the most recent daily reset (00:30 UTC) rather than your original starting balance, so the dollar amount you can lose in a day shifts as your balance grows or shrinks.

The 2-Step is the outlier: its 8% drawdown is an end-of-day trailing floor that rises with your highest closing-balance high — but it locks at your starting balance once the trailing math would put the floor there or higher. Breakout's own published example: a $105,000 high-water mark produces a floor of $97,000, i.e. the floor has already locked at the $100,000 starting balance rather than continuing to trail upward with your balance.

Frequently asked questions

Does Breakout's daily loss limit use my starting balance or a rolling balance?

A rolling balance — it's calculated as a percentage of your balance at the most recent daily reset (00:30 UTC), not your original starting balance. The dollar limit changes day to day as your balance changes.

What happens to Breakout 2-Step's trailing floor once I'm profitable?

It locks at your starting balance once your trailing high-water mark makes the floor reach that level — matching Breakout's own official example (a $105,000 high-water mark produces a $97,000 floor, locked right at a $100,000 starting balance). It never trails past that point.

Can I hold positions over the weekend or trade the news on Breakout?

Yes to both, per Breakout's rules — there's no restriction on weekend holding or news-event trading on any of the four plans.
