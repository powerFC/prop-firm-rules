How The5%ers' drawdown works

Both plans use a static drawdown in our data — a fixed percentage of your starting balance, set once and never adjusted. The 1-Step's floor is tighter (6%) than the 2-Step's (10%), which roughly offsets the 1-Step's single-phase structure against the 2-Step's two lower, split targets.

  • Max drawdown type (static vs. trailing) is assumed static pending confirmation — it isn't stated explicitly on The5%ers' own rules table.

Frequently asked questions

Does The5%ers' consistency rule apply after I'm funded?

It depends on the plan. On the 1-Step, yes — the 50% best-day rule applies both during evaluation and on the funded stage. On the 2-Step (8/5), our data shows no consistency rule during evaluation, but the funded stage is documented to carry the same 50% best-day rule — it isn't part of the evaluation-phase table above, which covers evaluation phases only.

Is there another target variant of The5%ers' 2-Step?

Yes — a 10%/5% target variant exists at checkout alongside the 8%/5% variant modeled on this page.

What's the payout split on The5%ers?

75/25 in the trader's favor, with a $2,000 payout cap on early cycles, and a maximum of 2 active accounts per trader.
