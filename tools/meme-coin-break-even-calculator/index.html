How this estimate works

Total cost = position × (1 + buy fee) + total fixed costs. Required reference exit value = total cost ÷ (1 − sell fee − estimated exit slippage). Required gain = (exit value ÷ position − 1) × 100.

Rates are converted from percentages. Fees and estimated exit slippage are modelled as additive deductions from the same reference exit value. This is the existing FuturesEdge meme app model. Buy-side execution loss is not separately modelled: use your actual acquired position value for retrospective calculations. Cashback is excluded until received. Do not subtract it twice by also entering a net fee.

Worked example

A $100 position, 1% buy fee, 1% sell fee, 2% estimated exit slippage and $0.50 fixed costs require $101.50 back. Dividing by 0.97 gives $104.64 of reference position value, or a 4.64% gain. Defaults are illustrative, not a quote from any platform.

Limits to keep in mind

This models one complete buy and sell. It does not predict fills, liquidity or price impact. Multiple entries, partial exits, token taxes and changing currency rates need separate treatment. Convert every fixed cost to the same currency. The optional market-cap result assumes price and market cap scale together at unchanged supply. Calculations use full precision; results are rounded for display.
